# Reference notes: what to follow and what to ignore

These images were generated by an AI model, so none of them is perfect. The
graphics reviewer must read this file and judge **reference fidelity** against
the "Follow" lists only. When an image disagrees with `docs/ART_BIBLE.md`,
the art bible wins.
The implementation checklist for these items is `docs/REFERENCE_FIXES.md`.

## 01_environment_hero.jpg: the master target ★
**Follow**
- The overall mood: golden-hour light from the upper right, soft long shadows, pastel sky haze.
- Pink frosting drips over golden dough cliffs, and the layered strata of the dough.
- Sprinkle-ball trees, the cruller-roof bakery with chocolate shingles and the voxel chimney steam.
- The candy-striped lighthouse with the glowing glazed donut, the wooden pier, and the amber syrup waterfall.
- Voxel density on the island: fine enough that the forms read as rounded.
- Palette balance: warm dough and pink dominate, with mint and green accents.

**Ignore / do better**
- The island here is a horseshoe on a flat cream slab. In the game it is a **complete ring**
  around a real **milk lagoon** with a surface, depth tint and reflections (game design §6).
- The clouds are much chunkier than the island. Game clouds use finer voxels and softer edges.

## 02_ground_level.jpg: use for mood and camera only
**Follow**
- Low camera height, warm sunset backlight, shallow depth of field in the background.
- Crumbs scattered around a bitten donut, and the lighthouse donut glowing on the horizon.
- Glazey's scale relative to the donut and bakery.

**Ignore / do better**
- The grass, flowers and bushes are **Minecraft-scale chunky blocks**. That is forbidden
  (art bible §2–3). Grass and flowers must be micro-voxels.
- Glazey's voxels are too large here. Use the character sheet's density.
- The bakery sign has text ("SWEET BAKERY"). Use no text in world art; use an icon (a donut) instead.
- The water is blue. The lagoon is **milk** (cream white, soft specular).
- The frosting ground looks like a flat plastic sheet. It needs voxel relief, drips and AO.
- The trees are generic green blobs. Use sprinkle-ball trees as in 01.

## 03_glazey_sheet.jpg: the canonical character ★
**Follow**

- Big glossy eyes with white highlights, a small happy mouth, and a 5-stripe rainbow sprinkle tuft.
- Stubby arms and feet, and soft rounded voxel forms.

- The progression to "perfectly round".

**Ignore / do better**
- The back view is pink, not coral. Keep the coral body color consistent from every angle.
- The side view has an odd extra limb. The silhouette is just two arms and two feet.
- "Normal" and "full" bellies barely differ. Game belly stages 0–5 must differ clearly.
- The text labels are sheet annotations only.

## 04_donut_lineup.jpg: the canonical donut set ★
**Follow**
- Each flavor's look: sprinkle pink, glossy chocolate, powdered jelly with red jelly showing,
  maple bar, powdered, translucent classic glaze, craggy blueberry, pastel swirl mystery,
  and the rainbow mega donut in the center.
- Frosting drips over the dough edge, the visible baked-dough band, and the glossy specular.
- **The bite mark on the classic glazed donut** (bottom left): a scalloped carve that shows
  the crumb interior, with crumbs on the table. This is the target for the Munch carve (M5).
- The studio look, which suits the asset gallery turntable (M3).

**Ignore / do better**
- The chocolate drip has stray green and rainbow artifacts.
- The blueberry glaze is nearly navy. Make it a softer, lighter blueberry (`#7C8CE0` family).
- The rainbow mega donut can glow, but it should stay just inside the palette rules
  (it's the one sanctioned "neon" item, used sparingly).
