# Review M5 iter 1 (reviewer r1)
## Verdict: FAIL

Commit reviewed: a6583d7. Harness OK on the GPU path (ANGLE Metal, Apple M4) with 18 files in `shots/M5/1/`. I viewed all the 1920x1080 frames, the 844x390 frames for bite-3 and the burp strip, every strip frame (f0 to f2), and zoomed crops of the faces, the bite and the crumbs. I read the reference images and `NOTES.md` first. The fidelity targets are K-07 (04, the scalloped bite with a crumb interior and crumbs on the table) and K-05 (03, the giant burp with the rainbow ring and the closed happy eyes).

Short version: the world and lighting carried over from M4 still hold up, and the burp is a real start. **The Munch itself, which is the reason this milestone exists, does not read yet.** The bite marks land on the wrong side of the donut. The crumbs are much larger than the donut's own voxels, by roughly 5 to 8 times on screen. Glazey isn't holding the donut. And his munch face in profile reads as a heavy black brow over a gaping lip, not as the "munching" expression on sheet 03.

## Blockers
- **[m5-bite-1, m5-bite-3, m5-crumbs-strip] Regression in shared content (§4 rule 5), the bite carve and the crumbs.** In `shots/M3/final/gallery-donut-bites`, the same classic-glaze donut has a deep, readable scalloped carve showing a dark, porous crumb interior, and the crumbs are fine, about 1-voxel grains (around 5 to 8 px).
  - In M5 the in-world crumbs are 20 to 35 px cubes at 1080p (bite-3: the cloud above the donut, upper-left at x 600–870, y 460–680, and the pile at its foot, x 600–900, y 800–900).
  - That is about 5 to 8 times the donut's own voxel pitch on screen (3 to 5 px), so they read as Lego bricks and dice next to a fine-voxel donut (art bible §2 and §3).
  - The bite-1 carve can barely be seen. It is a shallow nick on the donut's right rim, hidden behind Glazey's crumbs (x ~900–940, y 700–780).
  - Compared with M3, this is a visible quality drop in the same asset.

## Fix list (priority order; max 10; each concrete and testable)
1. **What:** The bites land on the side of the donut away from Glazey's mouth. In bite-3 both scallops sit on the far left rim (x 620–700, y 590–830), while his open mouth presses against the intact right rim (x ~950, y 620), and the crumb cloud bursts from the far side, not from his mouth. Cause and effect is broken: a 6-year-old won't see Glazey biting anything.
   **Where:** `src/game/Game.ts` `chomp()`. The carve angle is `atan2(HOLD.z, -HOLD.y) + k * MUNCH.turn` with `MUNCH.turn = 1.0` (`src/game/munch/Munch.ts:24`). Each bite moves 57° further around a donut that doesn't turn, so by bite 3 the carve is 115° away from the mouth.
   **Target:** Carve every bite at the rim facing the mouth, and turn the *donut* by the same amount between bites (in the opposite direction), so fresh rim comes round to the mouth. In bite-1 and bite-3 the newest scallop should touch his lips, and the crumbs should leave from that point.
2. **What:** The crumbs are far too large (the blocker above). **Where:** `Game.ts` `chomp()`, where `crumbs.emit({ size: [0.022, 0.04] })` uses 0.7 to 1.3 hero voxels, while the donut grid is finer (`e.voxel`). **Target:** Make the crumb size about 1 to 1.5 times `e.voxel`, so they are under 8 px at 1080p in the munch close-up. Use more of them (about 60 to 100) in 2 to 3 tones, including some darker toasted grains so they read against the cream glaze. They should match the M3 gallery crumbs and ref 04 bottom-left.
3. **What:** Glazey isn't holding the donut. It stands on its rim in the grass beside him (bite-1 and bite-3: the lower edge of the donut sits in the blades at y ~880). His arms hang at his sides; the near arm is a stub at x ~1190, y 650. Sheet 03 "munching" shows both stubby hands at the mouth, gripping the food. **Where:** `GlazeyRig.ts` and `GlazeyAnimator.ts` (the arm pose during the `munch` look), plus `HOLD` in `Game.ts:70`. **Target:** Both arms are raised and touching the donut rim, and the donut's lower edge is clearly off the ground with a soft contact shadow under it on the grass.
4. **What:** The munch and burp faces read wrong in profile. The closed-eye arcs are thick, near-black bars about 120 px long that look like a heavy brow (bite-1 x 950–1090, y 540–575). In burp f1 they slant down toward the centre, which reads as an **angry shout**, not the blissful burp on sheet 03. On the silhouette edge, the far eye shows as a dark notch (bite-1 x ~940, y 545; burp f1 x ~1160, y 715). The open mouth sits on the silhouette with a thick dark lip, so the whole face reads as a moustache. **Where:** the face looks in `src/art/models/glazey.ts` and the `munch`/`burp` looks in `GlazeyAvatar.ts`. **Target:** Use thinner (2 to 3 voxel) warm dark-brown (not black) "^ ^" arcs that curve *up* in every expression except surprise. Hide the far eye when its normal faces away from the camera. Pull the mouth one or two voxels in from the silhouette, and give the munch a cheek-puff bulge with crumbs at the lips, as on sheet 03.
5. **What:** The cream belly patch looks unlit and pasted on, like a sticker. It holds nearly one value from the lit side to the shadow side (roll: x 870–970, y 690–830, the same brightness as the sunlit coral, with no terminator), and it has a hard, stair-stepped border against the coral. In bite and burp it bulges out of the profile as a flat cream tongue under the mouth. **Where:** the belly material and palette flags in `src/art/palette.ts`, and the belly region in `glazey.ts`. **Target:** The belly shades with the body: wrap lighting with a visible terminator, darker on the shadow side, and one or two voxels of graded blend into the coral, as in the sheet 03 front view.
6. **What:** The munched donut's glaze has gone chalky and matte. In bite-1 and bite-3 the classic glaze is a flat, pale beige with no specular or fresnel rim. The M3 gallery and the M4 meadow donuts are clearly glossy. The bite's crumb interior (bite-3 left scallops) is nearly the same value as the glaze, so the carve doesn't read. **Where:** the edible donut's material when it is posed as "held" (it may lose its env/spec when reparented), and the carve's interior palette in `biteDonut`. **Target:** The glaze shows a visible specular highlight band at the camera-facing upper rim. The carve interior is at least 2 value steps darker and warmer than the glaze, with darker pores, as in ref 04 bottom-left.
7. **What:** The crumb motion doesn't read as a delightful arc (crumbs strip). In f0 the crumbs come out as a dense, clumped puff at the mouth. By f1 most of them hang in mid-air next to the face. By f2 a few lie at the donut's foot. There's no visible bounce or scatter fan, and the outcome (crumbs on the grass) is too sparse to read at 844x390. **Where:** the `VoxelParticles` emit parameters in `chomp()` (speed, up, drag 1.2, dir bias). **Target:** A fan of crumbs arcing out and down from the mouth. At least half of them land in a visible ring on the grass by f2, and some show a small bounce (art bible §8: bounce 1 or 2 times, then settle).
8. **What:** The burp's screen-space ripple can't be seen in any frame, and the hoop has no glow. Sheet 03's ring is a soft, luminous halo. Here the hoop is a dense, unlit pastel tube, and the ground ring's cubes are coarse (15 to 20 px at the front edge, f1 x 800–1700, y 760–1000), crawling over the frosting rim at the left (f1 x 790–900, y 860–900). **Where:** `src/engine/render/Ripple.ts` (amplitude 0.012 and band width 0.035), and the `FX_TUNING` emissive and cube size in `BurpWave.ts` (`cube: 0.034`). **Target:** A ripple you can see in f1 at full motion (about 6 to 10 px of displacement in the band). The hoop has a gentle emissive bloom inside the bloom threshold. Ground-ring cubes are under 10 px, and the ring hugs the lawn, not the frosting wall.
9. **What:** The framing of the munch shots hides the action. Glazey is in near-profile with his face turned away from the lens, the lighthouse donut, the glowing landmark, is cut off by the top edge, and the frame's centre of interest falls on the donut hole, not the bite. **Where:** `munchStage()` in `src/debug/scenes/m5Munch.ts` (`CAM.focusSide` and the camera yaw). Shot layout belongs to the builder, but the shipped munch camera should do the same. **Target:** A three-quarter-front view of Glazey's face and the bite together. The newest scallop sits on a thirds line, and the lighthouse donut is fully in frame on the horizon, like ref 02.
10. **What:** The roll still doesn't read as rolling, and the frame is weak. Glazey sits upright with no tilt, no motion squash and no dust trail. The upper 40 % is empty sky, and a cropped pink tree fills the left edge (x 0–230). **Where:** the roll pose in `GlazeyAnimator.ts`, and the roll stage in `m5Munch.ts`. **Target:** Some visible cue that he is rolling: a slight forward lean, a squash ellipse, and dust or grass puffs behind him. A tighter frame where he takes up about 25 to 30 % of the frame height.

## What is working (keep it)
- **The world.** Golden-hour key light from the right, cool sky fill, pastel haze, the frosting rim with drips over the dough band, sprinkle trees, the glowing bakery windows and the milk lagoon. This is the M4 baseline and it hasn't regressed.
- **The burp idea.** A rainbow hoop out of the mouth plus a rainbow ripple running across the lawn is legible and joyful in all three frames. The pastel band order (red → violet) matches K-05. Burp f0's surprised face (glossy eyes, white 2x2 glints, small "o" mouth) is the best Glazey expression in this gate.
- **The belly-5 ball silhouette.** It reads as "perfectly round", and the face is cute in roll: big glossy irises with highlights and pink blush.
- **The bite-3 carve shape.** Where it shows, it is a true multi-scallop voxel carve, not a decal (art bible §8). It just needs to be in the right place and in the right colours.

## Store-page test: no
The hero munch frame (m5-bite-3) shows a donut standing on its rim with its bite on the far side, a cloud of oversized beige dice floating beside it, and Glazey in profile with what looks like a black brow and a moustache. A store-page viewer wouldn't understand that he is eating it. The burp frame comes closer, but it isn't the gate's hero, and it isn't at store quality yet either (no glow, no visible ripple, an angry-looking face).

- **M3 final (donut bites, Glazey belly):** **regression.** The bite carve and the crumbs are coarser and less legible than `gallery-donut-bites`, and the glaze gloss is lost on the held donut. See the blocker and fixes 2 and 6.
- **M4 final (Glazey, the meadow at play):** the front face and eyes are unchanged (good, as in roll and burp f0). The new munch and burp face looks don't meet the M4 face's appeal (fix 4). This is new content, not a regression of an existing asset.
