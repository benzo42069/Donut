# Review M3 iter 1
## Verdict: FAIL

Captured by the reviewer: `npm run shots -- --gate M3 --iter 1` at commit `f037548` (SwiftShader path, 14 files in `shots/M3/1/`). The builder-supplied `shots/M3/pre/` was not used. All four reference images and `NOTES.md` were viewed. The most relevant references are 04 (donut lineup, studio look) and 03 (Glazey sheet).

This is a solid first pass in terms of breadth: 9 flavors, belly stages 0 to 5, a full prop set and real bite carves. It is not close to the bar. The studio presentation is flat and monochrome peach. The wooden stands overpower the donuts. Three of the nine flavors read as the same cream donut. The blueberry is a Minecraft staircase. Glazey's face, the most important asset in the game, is under-resolved and off-model. No shot meets the "every category ≥ 8" rule.

## Blockers
No strict art bible §10 instant-fail item was found. There is no z-fighting, shadow acne, debug overlay, gray default background or scary content. The gate still fails pass-bar items 2, 3 and 4 by a wide margin. The closest call on §10 is the blueberry donut, covered in fix 1. Its glaze has straight knife-slit tears through to the dough (centre-bottom of gallery-donuts, about x 965 and x 1050, y 660–740) and a pepper of single dark pit voxels across its top. At normal viewing distance these read as mesh tears and holes rather than authored cracks. If they are still present next iteration, they will be treated as a §10.1 blocker.

## Fix list (priority order; max 10; each concrete and testable)
1. **What:** Re-author the blueberry donut. Right now it is stepped terraces with dark 1-voxel AO lines on every step, the straight vertical slits show dough (about x 965 and x 1050, y 660–740), and dark pit voxels are scattered across the top. It is the only donut that looks like Minecraft and it breaks art bible §2 ("silhouettes read as smooth, rounded forms"). **Where:** `src/art/models/donut.ts`, the `craggy` form at about line 207 and `crackGlaze()` at about line 382. Also the per-voxel AO strength on 1-voxel steps (`src/engine/voxel/mesh.ts`). **Target:** Craggy lumps that read as soft, rounded swells like reference 04 (bottom middle). No straight slits; any cracks should be irregular, short and narrower than the glaze depth so dough never shows through as a line. No isolated dark pits. At 1080p, the silhouette should read as rounded, not stepped.
2. **What:** Glazey's eyes and mouth are under-resolved and off-model. Each eye is only about 6×7 voxels (`eyeW 3.0`, `eyeH 3.7`). It is painted into a curved surface with `z > front-2.5`, so the outline fractures into jagged spikes. Stage 0 reads as angry slanted "raccoon mask" eyes, and stages 1–2 grow stray lash spikes (gallery-glazey-belly, all six faces, about y 500–560). The mouth is a tiny dark slot. Compare reference 03, where the eyes are large clean round glossy domes with a crisp 2×2 white glint and a wide happy mouth. **Where:** `src/art/models/glazey.ts` lines 103–181. **Target:** Eyes roughly 1.6–1.8× larger with a clean circular outline at every belly stage. Paint them on a flattened face plane, or author them at 1/64 m sub-voxels. Keep a single 2×2 glint at the upper left plus the small secondary glint. Add a visible open smile with a tongue tint. No spikes, no asymmetry between the left and right eye, and the same eye shape on all six stages.
3. **What:** The flavors do not read apart. Powdered, jelly-filled and classic glazed (gallery-donuts, right column top and middle, and bottom-left) are three near-identical peach-cream donuts. The powdered sugar has no bright, rough, sparkly read; it is beige fuzz (art bible §5: "high-value, rough"). The classic glaze is opaque and matte-ish, with no translucency or fresnel showing dough through (§5 "semi-translucent look"). The jelly is a small red smear at the front, not a jelly window. **Where:** `src/art/palette.ts` (powdered, glaze entries), `src/engine/render/Materials.ts`, and the `LOOK` table in `donut.ts`. **Target:** Powdered reads near-white (`#FFF6E6` family) with high roughness and a speckled top-only dusting, and the dough clearly shows on the sides. The classic glaze is a thin, glossy, slightly see-through amber-cream layer where you can see the dough color beneath, with a fresnel rim. The jelly donut has a clear jelly opening with a glossy red bulge like reference 04 (top right). Pass condition: a grayscale crop of each of the three is distinguishable.
4. **What:** The studio presentation is flat and monochrome. The backdrop, floor, dough and stands all sit in one peach-to-brown value band, so the hero shot fails §4 "reads in grayscale". The subjects do not hold the strongest contrast. The key light is soft but weak; there is no warm-key/cool-fill split, no rim, and shadows are pale tan smudges. Compare reference 04, which has a creamy cloth backdrop with real folds, a brighter key, and clearly defined soft shadows under each stand. **Where:** `src/debug/scenes/studio.ts` (lights, `studioCream` background, backdrop at line 42). **Target:** A lighter, cooler cream backdrop (still warm, but higher value than the donuts' midtones) with visible cloth folds. Add a stronger warm key from the upper right with a cooler sky fill, so shadow sides of glaze go slightly cool. Add a subtle rim or back light to separate the donut tops from the wall. Contact shadows under each stand should be darker and tighter. The grayscale check should show the donuts as the highest-contrast forms in frame.
5. **What:** The serving stands dominate the frame. They are larger than the donuts, and their dark brown with heavy orange speckle (jitter reading as sesame noise) pulls the eye away from the subject in gallery-donuts, -bites and -low-tier. The concentric rings are also high-contrast lines. **Where:** `src/art/models/stand.ts`, and the wood jitter amplitude in `palette.ts`. **Target:** Stands about 15–20 % smaller in diameter and lower in value contrast, with the speckle reduced to the §4 ±3–6 % value range so the wood grain is a soft hint. The donut should be the brightest and most saturated thing on each plinth, as in reference 04.
6. **What:** The props have mixed fidelity and readability. The pier and fence (lower left) are coarse boxy planks next to blobby, smooth trees, so they do not look like one world. The green sprinkle tree (centre right) has a wet-plastic specular sheen, and its sprinkles look like random painted dashes rather than 1×1×3 capsules. The chocolate rock with white blotches (lower right) reads as a dirty, moldy lump, which is unappetizing. The crystal (centre bottom) is a smooth un-voxelled blob with no emissive or bloom, although §5 requires the choco-chip crystals to glow. The lighthouse donut lamp is unlit here. **Where:** `src/art/models/props.ts` and `lighthouse.ts`, the material roughness for foliage in `Materials.ts`, and `bloomThreshold 1.2` in `studio.ts` (line 167). **Target:** Foliage is matte (roughness ≥ 0.7), and the sprinkle capsules read individually, as in reference 01. The rock is a recognisable choco-rock with powdered-sugar caps on top faces only. The crystal and lighthouse lamp glow softly with bloom. The pier and fence planks have rounded ends and finer grain so their density matches the trees.
7. **What:** gallery-props composition. A large detached dark shadow floats on the backdrop wall, top centre (about x 1020–1190, y 220–390), cast by the green tree onto the cyc curve. It reads as a mystery blob in empty sky. Lighthouse shadows also streak up the wall at left-centre. The clouds sit at tree height, which flattens the scale. **Where:** `src/debug/scenes/gallery.ts` (props layout) and the cyc and shadow-receiver setup in `studio.ts`. **Target:** No prop shadows on the vertical part of the backdrop. Push the props forward, deepen the floor, or exclude the wall from receiving the tall props' shadows. Clouds should sit clearly above the props, and the bakery should be the focal point with the strongest contrast.
8. **What:** Glazey's body surface is lumpy and noisy, like oatmeal or knit, and the noise worsens with belly size (stage 5 at far right). The cream belly patch has ragged stair-stepped edges, and the blush is two hard flat pink rectangles. Reference 03 shows a smooth, soft, squishy body where the voxels show only up close. **Where:** `glazey.ts` (surface displacement and noise), and the normal smoothing for characters in `Materials.ts` and `mesh.ts`. **Target:** A smooth surface at gallery distance, with value jitter within ±3–6 %. The belly patch is a clean ellipse (anti-jagged at 1/32 m or 1/64 m). The blush is a soft oval with a lighter centre.
9. **What:** The rainbow mega donut (centre). The bands are flat and chalky with stair-stepped edges, the outer coral-red band dominates, and the center bowl's lilac reads as a plastic dish. The drips on the far inner wall clutter the hole (about x 920–1010, y 395–425). It does not have the luminous presence of reference 04, where it is the sanctioned glow item. **Where:** the `rainbow` entry in `donut.ts`, and its emissive and bloom in `Materials.ts`. **Target:** Even band widths with smooth borders, a slightly higher-value, glossier glaze with gentle emissive and bloom pop (within the palette rules), and a clean hole.
10. **What:** The maple bar (left middle) is a featureless orange-tan capsule. It has no drips, no visible dough band contrast and no maple sheen, so it reads like a plastic toy eclair. **Where:** the `bar` form and maple glaze in `donut.ts`. **Target:** A glossy amber maple top with 3–5 drips over the edge and a clear dough band, as in reference 04 (middle left).

## What is working (keep it)
- **Bite carves** (gallery-donut-bites): the scalloped two-lobe bites show a crumb interior with speckle, the glaze overhangs the bite edge, and a few crumbs sit on the stand and floor. This is the closest content to the reference (04, bottom-left) in the gate.
- **Pink sprinkle donut**: the glossy frosting, drips over the dough band and capsule sprinkles are good, and consistent with the M2 close-up.
- **Chocolate glaze**: it is dark, rich and has believable specular. The faceted, window-like reflections are slightly CG but acceptable.
- **Glazey belly progression**: stages 0–5 differ clearly in size, and stage 5 reads as "perfectly round" with closed bliss eyes. The coral body is consistent front, side and back in the turntable (it fixes reference 03's pink back). There are only two arms and two feet. The tuft has 5 stripes.
- **Bakery prop**: the cruller trim, chocolate shingles, donut sign instead of text, striped awning, flower boxes and lit windows have good charm.
- **Technical**: no acne, z-fighting, overlays or crawling edges. AA is clean.
- **Low tier** is nearly identical to high tier, which is good for the low-tier floor, but it inherits every issue above.

## Store-page test: no
The hero shot (gallery-donuts) would not survive next to reference 04, let alone the best indie games of the year. The frame is a monochrome peach soup in which the dark noisy stands are the strongest elements. Three donuts look the same, the blueberry looks like Minecraft, and nothing glows or sparkles. A premium store page needs the reference 04 feel: a creamy cloth studio, crisp key light and nine instantly distinct, mouth-watering flavors.
