# Review M3 iter 1 (reviewer r2)
## Verdict: FAIL

Captured by the reviewer: `npm run shots -- --gate M3 --iter 1-r2` → `shots/M3/1-r2/` (SwiftShader path, harness `ok: true`).
All 14 files viewed, plus the 2–4× crops I made of each shot's problem areas. All four reference images and `art/reference/NOTES.md` read first.
For the regression check I also captured `--gate M2 --iter m3reg-r2 --only m2-hero,m2-closeup-donut`.

Summary: this is a competent first pass on the asset library. The 3×3 lineup copies reference 04's layout, Glazey is coral from every angle, and belly stages 0–5 clearly differ. It is still well short of the bar. There are two technical blockers (shadow streaks in the lighthouse lantern and firefly pixels on Glazey's silhouette). Glazey's face is pixel-art rather than the big glossy eyes on the canonical sheet. Several donut materials miss their reference target: the classic glaze is opaque, the powdered donuts don't read as powder, and the chocolate shows terraced specular. The flat peach studio gives the cream-colored assets almost no value separation. This scores as solid indie work (about 6–7), not store-page work.

## Blockers
- **[gallery-props] Shadow-map streaks or acne in the lighthouse lantern** (upper-left third, about x 540–650, y 260–345 at 1080p). Dark, ragged vertical streaks run through the red cap, the cream lantern drum, the yellow glow band, the mint railing and the orange gallery floor. They don't follow any geometry: they cross the cap above the railing, so they can't be railing shadows. This is instant-fail §10.1 (shadow acne). The streaks will also crawl as the turntable rotates (§10.7).
- **[gallery-glazey-belly + strip f1/f2] Firefly or light-leak pixels on Glazey's silhouette.** Isolated pixels reach luma about 200 against neighbors around 110–150: 18 in the front shot and 43 in the back view. They cluster where arm meets body and along the lower body edge, e.g. (486,491), (209,506), (745,560) and (752,594) in the front shot. They are visible as white specks at full resolution and will shimmer in motion. This is instant-fail §10.1 (light leaks) and §10.7 (aliasing shimmer).

## Fix list (priority order; max 10; each concrete and testable)
1. **What:** Remove the lantern streaks. **Where:** `src/debug/scenes/studio.ts` key light (`shadow.bias = -0.0003`, `normalBias = 0.004`, `shadowExtent` 4 at `shadowMapSize`). The props shot is about 12 m wide, so the shadow camera likely needs a larger extent there, which lowers texel density. Also check `src/art/models/lighthouse.ts` for thin 1-voxel lantern mullions and railings that self-shadow. **Target:** the lantern cap, drum and gallery floor show clean, flat-lit faces with only geometric contact shadows at 4× zoom. Raise the normal bias or fit the shadow frustum per shot, or exclude thin, emissive lantern parts from casting shadows.
2. **What:** Kill the silhouette fireflies on Glazey. **Where:** likely specular aliasing on the smoothed character normals at grazing angles, or the rim light (`studio.ts` "Rim from the back left") hitting near-tangent normals and passing through SMAA. Check the character material in `src/engine/render/Materials.ts`. **Target:** zero isolated pixels brighter than their 2-px neighbors by more than 40 luma on any Glazey silhouette in the front, side and back frames. Clamp the specular or rim term at grazing angles, or apply specular anti-aliasing (roughness from normal variance).
3. **What:** Re-author Glazey's eyes, mouth and belly patch at higher face resolution. **Where:** `src/art/models/glazey.ts` (`eyeW = 3.0`, `eyeH = 3.7` voxels; the eye is an ellipse test on the body voxel grid). **Why:** the eyes are ragged, stair-stepped black blobs. At belly 0 and 1 the spiky "lash" voxels make them look like a scowling raccoon mask (lower-left two Glazeys). The canonical sheet (03) has big, round, glossy eyes with a dark-brown iris, a large white highlight plus a small secondary glint, and a clean, round, smiling mouth. The belly patch edge is a jagged pixel staircase, and at belly 4–5 the cream patch climbs up into the eye area. **Target:** use a 1/64 m face-decal grid on the front (or raise eye radius to about 5–6 voxels with a smooth round falloff). Eyes should read as perfect circles at 230 px character height, with a dark-brown iris (not near-black), a 2×2 glint plus a 1-voxel secondary glint, and gloss (low roughness) on the eye material. The belly patch should be a smooth ellipse that stays below the blush line at every stage.
4. **What:** Give the classic glaze its translucent look. **Where:** `src/art/models/donut.ts` (classic look) and the glaze material flags in `Materials.ts`. **Why:** the lower-left donut in gallery-donuts and the bottom row in gallery-donut-bites read as opaque cream fondant. Reference 04 and art bible §5 call for a lighter, semi-translucent glaze with fresnel rim where the golden dough shows through and the glaze thins at the edge. **Target:** the dough's golden color tints through the glaze on the top face (about 30–40 % of the hue shift), a visible fresnel rim on the silhouette, and thin glaze drips that let the dough band read clearly beneath.
5. **What:** Make the powdered donuts look powdered. **Where:** the powdered and jelly looks in `donut.ts`. **Why:** the upper-right and middle-right donuts look like cream fleece or meringue. They need high-value, rough, sparkly sugar dusted on top faces only (§5), with toasted dough showing on the lower flanks and the dough band. Reference 04 shows crisp white sugar grains over golden dough. The jelly also reads as a red blob stuck on the side rather than jelly oozing out of a split. **Target:** near-white rough sugar with per-voxel sparkle on top faces, golden dough visible on the lower 40 % of the body, and jelly seated in a dark split in the dough with glossy depth.
6. **What:** Remove the terraced specular rings on the chocolate (and slightly on the rainbow bowl). **Where:** normal smoothing of glossy materials (`Materials.ts`, mesher normals in `src/engine/voxel/mesh.ts`). **Why:** the top-center donut's specular breaks into concentric stepped contours that show the voxel staircase in the shading. **Target:** a continuous, soft, elongated highlight like reference 04's chocolate. Use smoother normals (wider kernel) on glossy flags only.
7. **What:** Fix the blueberry form and its cracks. **Where:** `donut.ts` `crackGlaze` and the 'craggy' form. **Why:** the bottom-center donut is the one asset that looks Minecraft-boxy: big terraced layers and a hard staircase silhouette. Its one long vertical crack (center front) and the notch on the right flank read as a mesh split or hole, not an old-fashioned crag. **Target:** a rounded, lumpy crag silhouette at the same smoothness as the other donuts, cracks as shallow, short, irregular fissures that read as glaze cracking (no deep, straight slots), and the color kept in the soft `#7C8CE0` family (this part is already good).
8. **What:** Fix the muddy bite interiors and the floating crumbs. **Where:** the bite-carve interior colors in `donut.ts`, and `scatterCrumbs` placement in `src/debug/scenes/gallery.ts`. **Why:** in gallery-donut-bites the carved crumb faces show dark gray-green mottled patches (center and right columns of both rows), which looks unappetizing, almost moldy. The two crumb piles sit about 150 px below the stands in empty floor at the lower right, one partly cropped at the frame edge. They have no contact shadow, so they look pasted on. **Target:** a warm, light, open-crumb interior (pale gold to cream with small toasted flecks, AO only in deep pockets), crumbs scattered on the stands and the floor right beside them, each with a visible contact shadow.
9. **What:** Lift the studio lighting so assets separate from the backdrop. **Where:** `studio.ts` (backdrop color and key/fill/rim balance). **Why:** in every shot, cream assets (powdered, classic, cream bakery walls, clouds) sit on a near-identical peach sweep. In grayscale the powdered and classic donuts nearly vanish (§4 value structure). The key is soft but low-contrast. Without shaped highlights and a stronger rim, the frame looks flat next to reference 04's warm, specular studio. **Target:** a cooler, lighter or slightly desaturated sweep (or a deeper warm gradient behind the subjects), a stronger warm key with defined specular, a visible cool rim on silhouettes, and every asset at least 15 % luma apart from the background directly behind it.
10. **What:** Fix the props shot composition and sprinkle placement. **Where:** gallery props layout (`gallery.ts`) and `props.ts` canopies. **Why:** the clouds cast a large dark blob shadow onto the backdrop wall in the center of the frame (about x 1020–1190, y 220–390), which reads as a smudge. The green and pink sprinkle-ball canopies have a wet-plastic, streaky specular and flat sprinkle rectangles lying on the surface, some outside the silhouette (left edge of the green tree). Reference 01's canopies are dense, matte sprinkle clusters. The pretzel trunk, chocolate rock and pier stair stringers are chunky staircases next to the smooth assets. **Target:** clouds don't cast shadows on the backdrop (or the wall shadow is softened and pushed out of frame), canopies are matte with sprinkles embedded and AO'd into the surface, and the trunk, rock and stair silhouettes match the smoothness of the bakery and lighthouse.

## What is working (keep it)
- The 3×3 donut lineup with the rainbow mega donut in the center and wooden stands follows reference 04's studio layout. The pink sprinkle donut is close to reference quality: glossy frosting, real drips, sprinkles set into the surface.
- The pastel swirl mystery donut and the maple bar are clean, appetizing and palette-true. The mega donut stays inside the palette rules, as NOTES asks.
- Glazey is coral from every angle, including the back (NOTES fix honored). There are only two arms and two feet in the side view. Belly stages 0–5 now differ clearly in silhouette, and the "bliss" closed eyes at stage 5 are charming.
- The bite carve is a real scalloped voxel carve with a frosting overhang, not a decal, and it reads instantly at three depths.
- The bakery (chocolate shingles, cruller ridge, donut emblem instead of text, glowing windows, striped awning) and the striped lighthouse match reference 01. Grass tufts and flower beds are true micro-voxels.
- The low-tier render is almost indistinguishable from high (about 33k pixels differ by more than 10 levels) and has no broken effects.

## Store-page test: no
The hero (gallery-donuts) is a clean asset sheet, but next to reference 04 it looks flatter and less appetizing. Half the donuts miss their material identity (opaque classic glaze, fleecy "powdered"), the chocolate shows stepped specular, the blueberry looks boxy, and the cream items dissolve into the peach backdrop. I would not put it on a premium store page next to the year's best indie games.
